Rhino Player **1.7.0** — a fuller continue strip, windowed Fill Screen, and smarter Lucky / catalog browsing.

### Highlights

- **Continue strip** — shows up to thirty recent videos; Escape leaves the grid and quits
- **I'm Feeling Lucky** — draws from the catalog with series-aware resume-or-first, episode grouping, and fresh cards after trash or remove
- **Folder neighbours in the catalog** — nearby videos in the same folder are indexed so search and Lucky can find them without scanning the whole tree
- **Fill Screen** — crop-to-fill when the picture does not match the window; also strips baked-in black bars for fill and thumbnails
- **Same-series next / previous** — skips unrelated titles in the folder on next, previous, and end-of-file
- **1080i / Bob** — local interlaced video plays at full field rate; Fill Screen stays steady through deinterlace
- **Homebrew on macOS** — opening the app from Finder or the Dock finds Homebrew GSettings schemas without a login-shell environment
